# Privacy Policy for Plants vs Animals

Last updated: 2026-10-14 (applies from version 1.2.0)

Plants vs Animals ("the game") is published by OEO GAMES on Google Play.

## Information on your device

Your game progress, scores, star ratings and settings (volume and difficulty) are saved on your
device. They are deleted when you uninstall the game or clear its data.

## Services the game uses

The game uses the following Google services. Each one collects data as described in its own
privacy policy; we do not sell any data.

- **Google Play Games Services** (leaderboards). If you are signed in to Play Games, your best
  score per level and your Play Games profile name are shown on public leaderboards. You manage
  your profile and its visibility in the Google Play Games app.
  [Google Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy)
- **Firebase Analytics and Firebase Crashlytics** (by Google). They record how the game is played
  (for example levels started, won or lost, and scores), basic device information, an app
  instance identifier, and crash reports, so we can fix problems and improve the game.
  [Firebase privacy information](https://firebase.google.com/support/privacy)
- **Google AdMob** (advertising). The game shows a rewarded video only when you choose to watch
  one to continue a lost level. AdMob may use your device's advertising ID, IP address and device
  information to show and measure ads. In the European Economic Area, the UK and Switzerland the
  game asks for your consent first, and you can change your choice at any time from
  Options > Privacy. [How Google uses data from apps](https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites)

You can reset or delete your advertising ID in your Android settings (Google > Ads).

## Children

The game is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect personal
information from them.

## Changes

If the game starts handling data in a new way, this policy and the Google Play Data safety
section will be updated before that version is released.
