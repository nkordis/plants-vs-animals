# Privacy Policy for Plants vs Animals

Last updated: 2026-10-13

Plants vs Animals ("the game") is published by OEO GAMES on Google Play.

## Information we collect

The game does not collect, store on our servers, or share any personal or device information.
It has no accounts, no advertising and no analytics.

Your game progress, scores and settings (volume and difficulty) are saved only on your device.
They are deleted when you uninstall the game or clear its data.

## Children

The game does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children.

## Changes

If a future version adds features that handle data (for example advertising), this policy will be
updated before that version is released, and the Google Play Data safety section will be updated
to match.
